## Batch Thesis

Videos 101-115 show the strong-uptrend version of CrypNuevo's playbook. BTC is pushing aggressively higher, so he does not blindly fade resistance. Instead he marks where retail stops are likely clustered, waits for the sweep, and then decides whether the sweep becomes continuation or a trap.

The batch adds three especially useful recognition rules:

- stop-loss zones above resistance are liquidity magnets;
- the 4h50EMA short trigger needs a close below and two 4h tests as resistance;
- momentum can make a short premature even after an upside target has been hit.

## Video-Level Trade Notes

### 101: These Stop Losses Will Be Next

The chart marks a resistance box with a stop-loss zone above it. The likely move is a stop run into that zone, confirmed by heatmap/order-book liquidity.

Entry is not the stop-loss zone itself. The trade comes after reaction:

- continuation if price accepts above the swept level;
- trap/reversal if price returns below and retests as resistance.

### 102: MM Is Trapping Retail

He frames the move through resistance as a market-maker trap. Retail shorts place stops above obvious resistance, price runs those stops, and then the key question is acceptance.

Short trigger: sweep above resistance, return below, retest failure.

Invalidation: price holds above the swept zone.

### 103: Is Money Switching Sides?

This video asks whether market makers are still building longs or starting to build shorts. The decision depends on resistance acceptance, trendline/EMA support, and weekly close behavior.

Continuation requires acceptance above resistance. Rejection from the high points back toward the prior support area.

### 104: Are Impatient Traders Next?

The setup is built around impatient shorts. If traders short too early under resistance, their stops become a target. He expects a sweep before any clean retrace unless structure breaks first.

Rule: high price is not enough for a short. Wait for failed acceptance, support loss, or EMA rejection.

### 105: Impatient Traders Got Rekted

The prior stop-loss sweep plays out. He uses order book, heatmap, and volume/profile-style views to check whether the upside target has been harvested.

Exit logic: take profit into the planned stop-loss/liquidity zone; reassess after the sweep instead of assuming immediate reversal.

### 106: M Pattern Coming Into Play?

The M-pattern is treated as a distribution structure, not a standalone drawing.

Short quality improves when:

- price fails above the range;
- the second high/sweep cannot hold;
- price loses the 4h50EMA/range support;
- there is downside liquidity below.

### 107: The Key Level In The Chart

The 4h50EMA becomes the key level.

For a short, he wants price to close below the 4h50EMA and then test it as resistance for two 4h candles. If price reclaims the 4h50EMA before that confirmation, the short thesis weakens or cancels.

### 108: Fading The BTC.D This Weekend

This is an altcoin filter setup.

Altcoin catch-up is possible only if:

- BTC holds support or remains stable;
- BTC dominance reaches resistance or starts fading;
- the altcoin chart shows support/reclaim.

Invalidation: BTC loses support or BTC.D breaks higher.

### 109: False Move Retraced

The weekend/false-move logic returns. A low-liquidity move that creates a CME gap is suspect; after the gap is filled, he rebuilds the map rather than continuing to fade blindly.

Confirmation: reclaim plus consolidation after the false move is retraced.

### 110: Choosing My Next Trade Based On Momentum

This is the core momentum-filter video.

He avoids shorting a vertical BTC pump while momentum is still strong. The better short requires momentum cooling, support loss, failed sweep, or EMA rejection.

If price consolidates above breakout support, continuation to the next liquidity pocket remains favored.

### 111: Will Altcoins Catch Up?

BTC has moved strongly, and altcoins may lag before catching up. He uses BTC strength, BTC.D behavior, and individual altcoin support/reclaim structures as filters.

The setup is not "buy alts because BTC pumped." It is "buy alts if BTC remains stable and dominance allows room."

### 112: Liquidations To The Upside

He maps a support box and an upside liquidation route. The route can include a dip/retest first, but if support holds, upside liquidations remain the target.

Invalidation: acceptance below support.

### 113: Best AI Crypto Tokens 2024

This is narrative/sector selection rather than BTC day trading.

He treats AI as a strong narrative, then filters tokens by market cap, tokenomics, exchange availability, relative strength, and chart quality. Even here, vertical FOMO is avoided; the better entry needs pullback, support, or reclaim.

### 114: Just One More Level

He looks for one more upside level after support/reclaim. The setup remains conditional:

- support/reclaim must hold;
- heatmap should support the upside pool;
- a pullback can be acceptable if it reacts.

### 115: HTF Liquidations At 74.1k

The higher-timeframe target is a liquidation pool around 74.1k, but the entry still depends on lower-timeframe support/reclaim.

Rule: HTF liquidation target is not an entry signal by itself.

## Reusable Strategy Patterns

### Stop-Loss Sweep Decision Tree

1. Mark the obvious retail support/resistance.
2. Infer where stops are clustered.
3. Check whether heatmap/order book confirms liquidity there.
4. Let price sweep.
5. If price accepts beyond the level, trade continuation.
6. If price returns through the level and retests, trade the trap.

### Two-Candle 4h50EMA Gate

For shorts:

- close below 4h50EMA;
- test it as resistance for two 4h candles;
- then look for continuation to lower liquidity.

For longs:

- reclaim 4h50EMA;
- hold/retest it as support;
- then look for range high or upside liquidity.

### Momentum Delay Rule

When BTC is moving vertically:

- do not short only because price is high;
- wait for momentum to cool;
- wait for support/EMA loss;
- or wait for a failed sweep and retest.

### BTC.D Altcoin Catch-Up Rule

Altcoin long setups need:

- BTC stable or strong;
- BTC.D fading/rejecting;
- altcoin support/reclaim;
- enough narrative or relative-strength reason to justify the trade.

## Practical Summary

The batch teaches that in a strong trend, liquidity above resistance often gets taken first. The trade is not the resistance touch; it is the reaction after the sweep. Continuation needs acceptance. Reversal needs failed acceptance. The 4h50EMA and momentum decide whether a short is real or too early.
