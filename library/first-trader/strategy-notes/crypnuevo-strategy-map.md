## Core Read

CrypNuevo is liquidity-first and bias-neutral. The profile sentence "Not a bull, not a bear. Just TA" matches the behavior in posts: he does not start with a directional opinion, he starts with where liquidity sits and whether price is accepting, rejecting, deviating, or filling a prior inefficiency/wick.

His roadmap usually looks like this:

1. Define the larger structure: range, trend, consolidation, HTF EMA retest, or macro context.
2. Mark obvious liquidity: long wicks, liquidation clusters, range highs/lows, prior highs/lows, CME/OI gaps, Bookmap clusters.
3. Watch price at an extreme: range high, range low, long wick, EMA, or liquidation pool.
4. Decide whether the move is acceptance, rejection, deviation, or wick fill.
5. Only then define execution trigger, invalidation, and target.

## Repeated Rules

- Long wicks are liquidity pools and price often revisits them.
- The 50% wick fill repeatedly appears as a practical first milestone; 100% wick fill is a fuller target when the broader context still supports it.
- Consolidation makes liquidation targets stronger; in ranges, liquidation clusters act like magnets.
- A range-high deviation followed by price moving back inside the range favors a move toward the opposite liquidity pool or range low.
- Several wicks in one direction without further progress can mean absorption or market-maker position building before reversal.
- EMAs matter as context, especially 50 EMA on 1h/4h/1W and 20/55 EMA for impulse continuation.
- Candlestick patterns need objective structure and confirmation. For hammer/shooting star, wick should be at least two-thirds of total candle, then the next candle confirms.
- Market sessions are part of the edge: Asia, London, and New York often form repeating patterns.
- Weekend and high-impact news conditions are dangerous unless there is a specific tested plan.

## Market Session Model

The pinned strategy thread gives the clearest session model:

- Pattern A: Asia holds, London pumps or dumps, New York reverses.
- Pattern B: Asia pumps or dumps, London holds, New York reverses.
- Asia pumps are treated with skepticism because he says they often retrace in later sessions.
- This session model is for normal weekday liquidity, not weekends or bank holidays.

Practical use: when reviewing a chart, do not only ask "what level?" Ask "what session produced this move, and is a later session likely to reverse or finish the liquidity run?"

## Setup Playbooks

### Setup 1: Range Extreme Deviation

- Conditions: price has been ranging or consolidating; price trades into range high/low or a liquidation cluster.
- Entry idea: wait for deviation above/below the range and return back inside, or retest/rejection of the range extreme.
- Trigger: lower-timeframe rejection, failed acceptance beyond the extreme, or a marked execution trigger.
- Invalidation: acceptance beyond the deviated high/low or failure of the retest/rejection thesis.
- Targets: opposite range liquidity, long wick liquidity, liquidation pool.
- Evidence: 2026 BTC updates around range highs, 79k/79.4k retest, and 71k liquidity pool; 2025 consolidation-range post.

### Setup 2: Wick Fill / Wick Magnet

- Conditions: a large prior wick remains unfilled or partially filled; market has no strong opposing confirmation.
- Entry idea: expect price to revisit the wick area because it represents liquidity.
- Confirmation: price starts moving toward the wick, or confluence from heatmap/liquidations/EMA/session behavior.
- Invalidation: strong acceptance away from the wick or structure shift that removes the liquidity thesis.
- Targets: wick base/tip or nearby liquidity cluster.
- Evidence: ETH "Fill the wick"; BTC March 2025 long-wick fill; July 2022 MM liquidity thread.

### Setup 3: EMA Supported Reversal After Absorption

- Conditions: price sits around 1h/4h 50 EMA or 1W50EMA; multiple wicks appear but price does not progress further.
- Entry idea: lack of progress after repeated wicks can signal market maker accumulation/distribution and possible reversal.
- Confirmation: reclaim/hold of EMA, session reversal, or candle confirmation.
- Invalidation: clean progression through the wick/EMA zone.
- Targets: nearest liquidity above/below, range midpoint/extreme, or prior structure.
- Evidence: 2024 BTC Tuesday update holding 4h50EMA; 2024 September update with 1h50EMA resistance and repeated downside wicks; 2025 1W50EMA retest.

### Setup 4: EMA/Fibonacci Impulse Continuation

- Conditions: impulse wave after corrective ABC; 20/55 EMA bullish cross; Fibonacci structure available.
- Entry trigger: break and retest of 0.618 as support; later prior-high break.
- Invalidation: failure to hold the 0.618 retest or loss of 20 EMA support during the impulse.
- Targets: prior high / Fib 1, then 1.618.
- Tools: log chart, log Fib, 20 EMA and 55 EMA, volume, RSI.
- Evidence: 2021 EMA/Fib impulse thread.

## Risk And Filters

- Do not trade weekends as if weekday session logic still applies.
- Do not trust Asia pumps blindly.
- Do not trade FOMC/CPI/news volatility without a specific strategy and risk plan.
- Do not enter simply because price touched a level; wait for reaction, confirmation, trigger, and invalidation.
- Cancel or flip a plan when fresh structure changes the setup, such as a new wick, fresh gap, news-driven break, or acceptance through the invalidation level.
- Do not force bullish/bearish identity. The correct question is where liquidity is and what price does there.
