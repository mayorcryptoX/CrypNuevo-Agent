## Repeated Trade Playbooks In Videos 026-050

### Playbook 1: Monday False Move

- Weekend creates a range.
- Monday makes a move that looks directional.
- He distrusts it unless price accepts beyond the range and EMA structure supports it.
- Later in the week, especially Wednesday/Thursday, a reversal can appear.

### Playbook 2: EMA-Gated Wick Fill

- Identify wick target.
- Check 1h50MA / 4h50MA / daily 50MA.
- If price is under resistance, wait for a short-squeeze/retest before shorting.
- If resistance flips to support, quit short idea and favor higher targets.

### Playbook 3: Range-High Deviation Short

- Mark range high and upper liquidity.
- Wait for sweep/deviation above range.
- Wait for return inside range and consolidation.
- Enter short only after the return inside is accepted.
- Target range lows.

### Playbook 4: Liquidity Sweep Long

- Price sweeps lower liquidity or range lows.
- Reclaims the range or EMA.
- Retests reclaimed level as support.
- Long toward mid-range, range quarter, range high, or remaining upper liquidity.

### Playbook 5: Altcoin Weakness / Swing Shorts

- BTC dominance rises or breaks out.
- Altcoins fail range support or remain weak during BTC moves.
- Altcoin rallies inside the range can become short opportunities.
- Do not assume altcoin bottoms before BTC.D supports the idea.

## Trade Management Rules

- Do not treat a correct target as an entry.
- Define target, entry, take profit, and invalidation before trade.
- If plan invalidates, remove drawings and rebuild.
- Do not short resistance blindly when upper liquidity remains.
- Do not long support blindly when price is accepting below it.
- Use 1h/4h50EMA reclaim/retest as a directional switch.
- Friday/weekend BTC trades require extra caution; he often avoids them.
- Macro events can create new wicks and liquidity pools that become future targets.
