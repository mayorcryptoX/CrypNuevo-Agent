## Strategy Patterns Added By This Batch

### Pattern 1: EMA Gate

Use the 1h/4h50EMA as a binary execution gate:

- reclaim and hold = long route can activate;
- reject and close below = short route can activate;
- intrabar touch alone is not enough;
- 4h close matters when the 4h50EMA is the setup level.

### Pattern 2: 50% Wick Decision

Use 50% wick fill as a reaction test:

- hard rejection = take profit or consider reversal;
- consolidation = likely continuation to 100%;
- loss of 1h50EMA after rejection = short/retrace route.

### Pattern 3: News Second Structure

On FOMC/CPI/event days:

- avoid pre-event entry;
- let the first candle create traps and imbalances;
- wait for the second structure;
- trade reclaim/retest/rejection only after volatility gives a readable level.

### Pattern 4: Manipulation Retest

After a sweep of a psychological/prior high:

- do not short the pump immediately;
- wait for momentum shift;
- wait for retest of swept level;
- enter on rejection;
- target nearest EMA/support/liquidity, often 15m50MA.

### Pattern 5: Support-Box Reaction Scaling

Inside a support box:

- small entry is acceptable only when it is a planned zone;
- compound only after stop-hunt wick plus reaction;
- exit/stand aside if price sits inside the box with no bounce;
- do not average blindly into acceptance below support.

## Recognition Checklist For Future Charts

- Is the trade a swing idea or a day-trade trigger?
- Which 50EMA is the active gate: 1h, 4h, daily, or weekly?
- Has price swept liquidity, or is it only approaching it?
- Did price reclaim and consolidate, or only wick?
- Is 50% wick fill acting as rejection or acceptance?
- Is the target the nearest realistic pool or a distant heatmap level?
- Is the current session likely setting a trap or confirming one?
- Is there a news event where the first candle should be ignored?
- Where does the idea fail on candle close, not just wick?
- Where is the planned exit before entering?

## Practical Summary

For recognition, this batch can be compressed into one rule:

CrypNuevo maps the route first, then waits for a level to behave correctly. The behavior he wants is sweep -> reclaim/retest -> EMA or range acceptance -> liquidity/wick target. The behavior that cancels the idea is acceptance beyond the invalidation level, especially a candle close through the active 50EMA gate.
