## Purpose

This note turns the first 50 videos into recognition rules for entries, confirmations, exits, and invalidations. It is designed for chart review: when a future BTC or altcoin chart appears, use this matrix to decide which CrypNuevo pattern it resembles.

## Core Workflow In The First 50 Videos

1. Define the market state: range, trend, deviation, consolidation, or retest.
2. Mark obvious liquidity above and below price.
3. Mark long wicks, CME/OI gaps, and prior highs/lows.
4. Identify the active 50EMA context, especially daily/1h/4h.
5. Check session and weekly psychology: weekend range, Monday false move, London hold, New York reversal, midweek reversal.
6. Decide whether the level is a target or an entry.
7. Wait for sweep, reclaim, retest, rejection, or acceptance.
8. Define invalidation before the trade.
9. Take profit into liquidity, wick fill, EMA, mid-range, or opposite range edge.
10. If invalidated, rebuild the plan from a clean chart.

## Entry Patterns

### 1. Sweep Then Reclaim

Best used for:

- range-low longs;
- support-box longs;
- weekend/Monday false move reversals;
- CPI/FOMC second move.

Required behavior:

- price trades below the obvious low/support;
- sellers do not get continuation;
- price closes or consolidates back above the level;
- retest holds.

Avoid:

- buying the first touch of support;
- buying while price is still accepting below the level.

### 2. Sweep Then Failed Retest

Best used for:

- range-high shorts;
- wick-fill shorts after upper liquidity sweep;
- manipulation/breakout traps.

Required behavior:

- price trades above obvious high/resistance;
- continuation fails;
- price returns below the level;
- retest from below rejects.

Avoid:

- shorting directly into vertical momentum before the retest.

### 3. EMA Reclaim

Best used for:

- day-trade long after lower wick;
- swing continuation after reclaiming daily/1h/4h50EMA;
- mid-range continuation.

Required behavior:

- price closes above the relevant EMA;
- retest holds as support;
- liquidity above is clear enough to justify reward.

Avoid:

- entering because price touched the EMA intrabar.

### 4. EMA Rejection

Best used for:

- short after failed reclaim;
- pullback continuation;
- wick-fill down toward lower liquidity.

Required behavior:

- price trades into the EMA from below;
- it cannot close/accept above;
- rejection candle or lower-timeframe breakdown appears;
- target below is nearby and clean.

Avoid:

- shorting if the candle closes above the EMA and retests it as support.

### 5. News Second Structure

Best used for:

- CPI;
- FOMC;
- Powell speech;
- surprise ETF/news catalyst.

Required behavior:

- first move creates trap/liquidity;
- wait for volatility to slow;
- trade the second reclaim/retest/deviation.

Avoid:

- guessing the number;
- entering before the event;
- chasing the first candle.

## Confirmation Patterns

### Candle Acceptance

A wick through a level is not enough. He wants to know whether price accepts:

- above resistance;
- below support;
- back inside the range;
- outside the range.

Acceptance is usually shown by close, retest, and consolidation.

### 50EMA Behavior

The first 50 videos repeatedly use 50EMA as a structural pivot:

- daily 50EMA for larger BTC bias;
- 1h/4h50EMA for execution and retest;
- weekly 50EMA for bull/bear market context when available.

### Session Behavior

Session confirmation matters when the setup is time-sensitive:

- Asia move can be a trap;
- London should hold/range if New York reversal is expected;
- New York should confirm with stop-hunt/reclaim or failed continuation.

### Liquidity Confirmation

Liquidity tools are not standalone entries. They confirm:

- a visible chart level;
- an unfilled wick;
- a range high/low;
- an OI/CME gap;
- an EMA or session route.

## Exit Patterns

### First Take Profit

Common first TP areas:

- 50% wick fill;
- mid-range;
- nearest 1h/4h50EMA;
- closest liquidation cluster;
- prior high/low.

### Final Take Profit

Common final TP areas:

- 100% wick fill;
- opposite range edge;
- large liquidation pool;
- range high/low after mid-range breaks;
- HTF resistance/support.

### Protective Exit

Exit early or reduce when:

- price reaches target but momentum stalls;
- session window ends;
- event risk is approaching;
- liquidity disappears;
- price consolidates against the idea inside the entry zone.

## Invalidation Patterns

### Level Acceptance Against Thesis

The strongest invalidation is not a wick. It is acceptance:

- above range high for a deviation short;
- below range low for a deviation long;
- above EMA for an EMA rejection short;
- below EMA for an EMA support long.

### No Reaction In The Zone

If price reaches a support/liquidity box and sits there without bouncing, the idea weakens. This is especially important for planned longs.

### Session Failure

If London does not hold after Asia movement, the New York reversal thesis weakens. If New York accepts in the same direction as the trap move, the reversal idea is cancelled.

### News Acceptance

After CPI/FOMC, if price accepts beyond the planned level after volatility settles, the old route is invalidated. The chart must be rebuilt.

## Pattern Recognition Shortcuts

- Long wick plus range context: look for 50% wick fill first.
- Missed liquidation zone by a small amount: expect price to revisit after liquidity builds.
- Breakout with weak volume into known resistance: suspect deviation.
- Fast news candle through both sides: wait; the second structure matters.
- Weekend range followed by Monday move: assume false move risk until London/New York confirms.
- Altcoin support box while BTC.D is rising and BTC is weak: do not buy blindly.
- Price at mid-range: reduce conviction; wait for edge or reclaim.
- Price at range extreme with liquidity beyond it: prepare sweep/reclaim or sweep/reject setup.
- Daily 50EMA near mid-range: stronger reaction area and likely TP/retest zone.

## First-50 Practical Rule

The first 50 videos can be summarized as:

Do not trade the target. Trade the reaction at the level that makes the target valid.

For CrypNuevo-style recognition, the best setup is not "liquidity above" or "liquidity below." It is a complete chain:

liquidity target -> sweep or reclaim -> EMA/range/session confirmation -> planned entry -> nearest TP -> invalidation.
