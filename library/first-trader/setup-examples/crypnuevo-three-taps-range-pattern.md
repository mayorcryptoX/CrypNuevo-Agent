## Summary

CrypNuevo names and explains the **Three Taps Pattern** — a range behaviour where price visits the same extreme three times before a sustained move. The second and third taps can be a clean retest OR a deviation below/above the extreme; the order does not matter. Deviations at the third tap are explicitly liquidity-mechanical: they hit the stop losses clustered just beyond the range low/high and trick breakout traders into positions that then get reversed.

He is actively planning this pattern on BTC as of June 15 2026: long open, targets mid-range $69k for the close, then re-orders at range lows for the third tap entry.

## Extracted Strategy

**Pattern name:** Three Taps Pattern (his own term, used without hedging — treat as a named, repeated concept)

**Structure:**
- Price is in an established range
- Tap 1 = first visit to the range extreme (range low for a long setup)
- Tap 2 = second visit — either a clean retest or a deviation below the extreme
- Tap 3 = third visit — the other of the two (retest or deviation); order between tap 2 and tap 3 is irrelevant
- After all three taps, the range low holds and price rotates toward the mid-range or range high

**Liquidity logic behind the deviation tap:**
- Deviations exist to sweep the stop losses sitting just beyond the obvious range extreme
- They also trap breakout traders who enter short on the "break"
- Once stops are taken and breakout traders are positioned wrong, price reverses — this is the entry signal
- Direct quote: "the deviation is to efficiently hit stop losses and trick breakout traders. Common range behaviour."

**Setup conditions (inferred from his current BTC plan):**
- Must be in a clearly defined range with identifiable high and low extremes
- Two prior taps already visible; third tap anticipated
- At the third tap: watch for deviation sweep then reclaim, or clean retest with momentum loss
- Entry: after the tap/deviation confirms (sweep + return inside range, or rejection candle at the level)
- Close/TP for existing longs at mid-range if momentum is decreasing and a rejection appears

**Current application (Jun 15 2026, BTC):**

## Confirmation And Invalidation

**Confirmation signals at the third tap:**
- Deviation sweep below range low → price returns inside range (reclaim) on a higher-timeframe close
- Or: clean retest of range low with visible momentum loss (candle close, waning volume, rejection wick)
- Mid-range recovery after tap = first evidence the pattern is playing out

**Invalidation:**
- Acceptance below the range low (candle close below, consolidation below) = pattern fails, range broke
- Not stated explicitly in this thread, but consistent with his standard rule: "acceptance below key level = invalidation"

## Risk And Psychology

- Pattern is not a signal at touch — it requires the tap/deviation to confirm before entry
- This is range behaviour; mid-range entries remain "no man's land" (don't enter between the taps)
- The deviation specifically exists to trap impatient traders — waiting for confirmation after the deviation is essential
- He closes longs into the mid-range rather than holding through it, consistent with his standard TP-at-liquidity rule
