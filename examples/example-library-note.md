## Summary

The example source teaches that range trades need context, patience, and clear invalidation. It warns against entering in the middle of a range when price has not reached liquidity, support, or resistance.

## Extracted Strategy

- Identify the range high and range low.
- Avoid mid-range entries unless there is a strong lower-timeframe trigger.
- Prefer reactions at extremes or clean reclaims/rejections.

## Confirmation And Invalidation

- Confirmation: reclaim and hold, or rejection from a key level.
- Invalidation: acceptance back through the level that supported the idea.

## Risk And Psychology

- Do not trade because price is moving.
- Define the invalidation before sizing.
