## Main Pattern Found

This batch is the clearest formalization so far of his range-deviation playbook.

He repeatedly divides the active BTC range into:

- range high;
- range low;
- mid-range;
- range quarters.

The range quarters are not decorative. He uses them as support/resistance checkpoints, continuation targets, and areas where a reclaim/retest can provide a trigger.

## Exact Strategy Elements

### Range Deviation Strategy

The recurring sequence is:

1. Define the range high, range low, mid-range, and quarters.
2. Identify liquidity above/below the range, especially liquidation heatmap bars and obvious stop zones.
3. Wait for price to take liquidity outside one end of the range.
4. Do not short/long the touch blindly.
5. Wait for price to return inside the range.
6. Confirm consolidation back under resistance or above support.
7. Target the mid-range first, then opposite range liquidity or a breakout through the opposite side.

In videos 047-055, this becomes highly explicit around the 31.7k-31.8k upside liquidity area. He wanted BTC to take upside liquidity, return inside the range, consolidate under the range highs, then offer a swing short toward mid-range, range lows, and possibly below-range liquidity.

### Wick-Fill Rule Gets More Precise

The wick-fill rule is repeated and sharpened:

- the 50% wick fill is the minimum expected target;
- after 50% is filled, the wick can be considered "filled enough";
- 100% fill remains possible if price consolidates and fails to react;
- if a new wick is created by a news move or session move, that wick becomes a fresh target.

Video 050 is especially useful because he states that the system expects at least the 50% wick fill before he considers the range exit thesis mature.

### Session Rule

The Asia/London/New York rule is now expressed as part of the trading system, not just observation:

- Asia pumps are distrusted.
- An Asia pump often gets retraced within 24 hours.
- If New York does not retrace it, the next Asia session can.
- London holding after an Asia move can support continuation into New York.
- A New York stop-hunt at the start of the session can create the actual trade.

### CPI / FOMC Rule

Video 052 adds a concrete event-day process:

- do not guess the CPI number;
- do not trade immediately into the release;
- wait at least 30-45 minutes after the data;
- expect a false first move;
- trade the reclaim/retest if price deviates outside the range and returns inside.

For a positive CPI scenario, he still does not blindly buy. He wants the chart to prove itself through deviation/reclaim or support at the 4h50EMA/mid-range.

### Altcoin Short Framework

Videos 043 and 046 expand the altcoin logic:

- altcoins can keep falling after BTC has already found relative strength;
- BTC dominance rising while BTC drops is hostile for altcoins;
- altcoin rallies inside a larger bearish/range structure can be used for swing-short setups;
- many older-cycle altcoins may not survive or may lag badly, so he does not assume old highs return.

This is not just "alts are weak." The key filter is BTC direction plus BTC dominance. If BTC drops and BTC dominance is high/rising, altcoins are expected to underperform.

### Liquidity-Map Interpretation

He repeatedly compares:

- chart levels;
- Hyblock liquidation levels;
- liquidation heatmap;
- order book red/green bars;
- open-interest gaps.

A subtle rule appears in videos 051-054: if price comes close to a large liquidation zone but does not fully take it, he often interprets that as the market maker building more liquidity there for a later sweep.

## Evolution From Earlier Batches

Earlier videos already had liquidity, wicks, EMA, and session behavior. This batch adds stricter execution architecture:

- ranges are subdivided into quarters;
- deviation trades require return inside range and consolidation;
- news trades require waiting after release;
- false first moves around CPI/FOMC become expected behavior;
- volume is used occasionally as confluence for a false breakout;
- DXY/SPX lag is used as a warning, not a standalone entry.

The system is becoming less "target reached, next level" and more "conditional route map with two-sided scenarios."

## Reusable Playbook

### Setup: Deviation Above Range High Into Swing Short

- Conditions: BTC inside a clear range; high-side liquidation zone above range high; euphoric sentiment or obvious upside stops.
- Trigger: sweep/deviation above range high, return inside range, consolidation under range high or failed reclaim.
- Confirmation: poor daily close through resistance, low volume on breakout candle, repeated upside wicks/large green candles retraced, Hyblock/order-book liquidity now lower.
- Targets: mid-range first, range low second, below-range liquidation/wick if momentum continues.
- Invalidation: reclaim range high and hold as support, especially with strong volume and continuation.

### Setup: Deviation Below Range Low Into Long

- Conditions: macro/news volatility or stop-hunt below range low; liquidity/wick target above remains unfilled.
- Trigger: price returns inside range and retests range low/support.
- Targets: mid-range, range quarter, 50% wick fill, full wick, upside liquidity.
- Invalidation: acceptance below range low.

### Setup: 50EMA + Mid-Range Continuation

- Conditions: 1h/4h 50EMA aligns with mid-range or range quarter.
- Trigger: reclaim and retest of 50EMA/mid-range as support.
- Targets: next range quarter, range high, wick-fill target, liquidation zone.
- Invalidation: loss of the reclaimed 50EMA/mid-range with acceptance.
