## Strategy Additions

### Weekly Psychological Pattern

Several videos describe a recurring sequence:

1. Weekend range forms in low liquidity.
2. Monday produces a false move, often into resistance/liquidity.
3. Asia pumps are distrusted.
4. London must hold for a New York reversal setup.
5. A midweek reversal can bring price back into or near the weekend range.

This is distinct from the simpler Asia/London/New York model. It adds a weekly structure around the session model.

### Monday False Move

Videos 27, 28, 29, and 39 repeatedly discuss Monday false moves. He uses:

- weekend range,
- Asia pump/dump,
- London holding,
- New York stop-hunt,
- liquidity above trendlines/resistance,
- 4h/daily 50EMA resistance.

### Plan Invalidation And Reset

Video 30 is important because he says the second part of his projection was wrong and invalidated, then explains that the only way forward is to remove drawings and make a new plan.

This is strong evidence for his anti-bias mentality:

- he does not defend an invalid plan;
- he rebuilds around current price, DXY, liquidations, and levels.

### Order Book vs Liquidation Map

Video 29 explains that the order book includes buy/sell limit orders, stop losses, and liquidations, but it does not identify which is which. He then uses Hyblock liquidation charts and chart structure to infer what kind of liquidity is present.

This is a more nuanced version of the liquidity model.

### 20/50 Weekly EMA Bull/Bear Framework

Video 32 introduces a higher-timeframe bull/bear market framework:

- 20MA crossing below 50MA can signal bear market conditions.
- 20MA crossing above 50MA can support bull market conditions.
- He says he will not call a bull market until sufficient signal/confluence appears.

This adds a macro trend filter separate from the daily/1h/4h 50 EMA trading tools.

### Altcoin Phase Continues

The Solana video keeps the altcoin-bull-market preparation theme:

- high-timeframe altcoin zones,
- BTC dominance as key risk filter,
- concern that BTC dominance breakout would be bad for altcoins.
