## Main Strategy Findings

### Higher-Timeframe To Lower-Timeframe Format

By videos 123-127 he explicitly describes a newer style:

- start from weekly/daily structure;
- mark the main support/resistance and range;
- then move into 4h/1h for triggers;
- separate swing-trade levels from day-trade levels;
- prepare scenarios in advance instead of reacting emotionally.

This is a maturity step from daily "today's setup" videos into a complete market map.

### Indicator Encodes The Playbook

Video 122 confirms what he considered core enough to automate:

- wick-fill strategy with 50% and 100% levels;
- liquidity pools based on large candle bodies;
- trading sessions;
- multi-timeframe 50MA;
- toggles for showing/hiding each module.

He explains that the indicator is not magic. It prevents missed context and helps him see wicks/liquidity pools faster. The trading decision still comes from structure, reaction, and trigger.

### 50MA Hierarchy

Videos 117, 118, and 123 strengthen the 50MA filter:

- weekly 50MA is the macro bull/bear and support/retest reference;
- daily 50MA is a swing filter;
- 4h/1h50MA is the execution layer;
- he avoids new longs while BTC is below or rejecting the daily 50MA;
- daily 50MA reclaim plus retest is required before higher-confidence longs.

### Deviation Back Into Range

Videos 123-124 give one of the clearest range-deviation explanations:

- downside range break with negative news can be a deviation, not necessarily trend continuation;
- reclaiming back inside the range confirms the deviation;
- a long wick below the range is an imbalance and often at least a 50% fill target;
- a CME gap may remain open for weeks/months, so it is evidence but not an immediate reason to fade.

### Swing Vs Day Trade Separation

Videos 123 and 125 make the separation practical:

- a day trade can target a bounce from low to resistance or EMA;
- a swing long needs reclaim of a major level, often a range low/high or daily 50MA;
- a move can be day-trade territory while still not enough confirmation for a swing;
- swing targets come from wider range highs, OI gaps, HTF liquidity, or major wick fills.

### Spot Buying Framework

Video 126 is the clearest spot-buying framework so far.

Spot interest improves when:

- BTC is near range low or HTF support instead of mid-range chop;
- ETH and altcoins show relative strength or reclaim patterns;
- BTC dominance supports alt recovery;
- the market offers a discount but price is not accepting lower;
- the buying zone has a clear invalidation.

### Retail Flush And Channel Break

Video 128 continues the market-maker flush theme:

- price consolidates in a visible channel;
- retail builds positions around channel edges;
- a flush takes out one side;
- he watches whether price reclaims the channel/range or continues to the next lower liquidity.

The channel drawing identifies likely positioning; it is not the entry by itself.

### Altseason Timing

Video 129 expands altcoin logic:

- BTC structure must not be at immediate breakdown risk;
- BTC.D must reject or stop rising aggressively;
- TOTAL3/altcoin market-cap structure needs support/reclaim;
- individual altcoin charts must have clean support, reclaim, or range breakout;
- sector narratives can help, but chart structure comes first.

### Long Wick To Fill

Video 130 returns to the wick-fill model in a larger market-outlook context.

Rules remain consistent:

- a large wick is an imbalance/magnet;
- 50% fill is the first practical target;
- 100% fill requires continuation context;
- support/reclaim below the wick target is needed for a higher-probability route;
- if price rejects the wick-fill zone, reassess rather than forcing continuation.

## Exact Setup Library Additions

### Setup: Indicator-Assisted Liquidity Map

- Conditions: chart has multiple possible wicks/liquidity pools and the manual map is noisy.
- Use: enable wick-fill, liquidity pools, sessions, and multi-timeframe 50MA to avoid missing context.
- Entry trigger: still requires reclaim, rejection, EMA hold/loss, or range acceptance.
- Invalidation: indicator level is invalid if price accepts through the structural level against the thesis.

### Setup: Spot Buying Opportunity

- Conditions: BTC at HTF/range support, ETH/alts showing relative strength, BTC.D not pressuring alts, and market panic/discount is visible.
- Entry trigger: support reaction, reclaim, or consolidation above the buying zone.
- Targets: range midpoint/high, prior resistance, or sector-relative-strength target.
- Invalidation: acceptance below the spot zone or BTC/BTC.D context flips against alts.

### Setup: Range Deviation Reclaim

- Conditions: price breaks below/above a mature range and sentiment/news supports continuation.
- Entry trigger: price returns inside the range and holds/retests the range boundary.
- Targets: mid-range, opposite side of range, wick-fill level, or OI gap.
- Invalidation: boundary retest fails and price accepts outside the range again.

### Setup: Long Wick Market Outlook

- Conditions: large HTF wick remains partially unfilled and broader market context supports retrace.
- Entry trigger: support/reclaim below the wick target, or consolidation after 50% wick fill.
- Targets: 50% wick fill first, 100% only if continuation structure holds.
- Invalidation: rejection at 50% or loss of support/reclaim path.
