## Main Pattern Found

This batch turns the BTC-only range/deviation framework into a cross-market decision process.

He still starts with BTC structure, liquidity, wicks, EMAs, and sessions, but now he repeatedly checks:

- DXY structure and possible deviation around psychological levels;
- SPX direction;
- EUR/USD and other FX pairs as dollar-context clues;
- BTC dominance for altcoin risk;
- order-book liquidity and heatmap targets;
- weekly candle close relative to range lows/highs.

## Exact Strategy Elements

### Cross-Market Confirmation

The DXY is no longer just background. He uses it as a route filter:

- if DXY reclaims a key level and holds support, BTC downside becomes more likely;
- if DXY fails a reclaim/deviation, BTC can recover;
- BTC lagging while DXY/SPX move is a warning that the BTC move may come later;
- DXY around psychological levels, especially 100, can create deviation traps.

He does not use DXY as an automatic signal. It must match BTC liquidity and structure.

### Range Deviation Confirmation

Videos 056-070 repeat the batch-005 range-deviation model and add weekly confirmation:

- deviation is confirmed after price returns inside the range and consolidates;
- after a confirmed deviation at one end, he expects a move toward the other end;
- if price is below range lows, the weekly close matters;
- a weekly close back inside the range opens the door to mid-range;
- repeated weekly closes below range lows/testing resistance favor further downside.

### Order Book / Heatmap Targeting

Video 060 explicitly frames the order book as a way to see where BTC might go next.

He compares:

- limit orders;
- stop losses;
- liquidation zones;
- heatmap brightness;
- Hyblock liquidation clusters.

He treats clustered order-book/heatmap liquidity as target evidence, especially when it aligns with chart support/resistance or old range levels.

### Weekend And Weekly Timing

He repeats:

- Friday/New York is often the last useful active session before weekend;
- weekends usually have low liquidity and tight ranges;
- if a target is not hit by Friday/New York, it can be postponed into next week;
- weekly candle close can decide whether a reclaim is meaningful.

### FOMC / CPI Process

The news process remains procedural:

- pre-FOMC: understand market pricing, expected hike/hold, and Powell press conference;
- avoid trading until at least 30 minutes after Powell finishes;
- day before CPI/FOMC often consolidates;
- CPI can trigger midweek reversal;
- news first move can be a stop-hunt or false move.

### Altcoin Buy-Zone Work

Video 064 is not a blind altcoin-buy video. It is a high-timeframe zone-preparation video.

The rules:

- first check BTC structure and BTC dominance;
- if BTC is below daily 50EMA and rejecting range lows as resistance, altcoin buys need patience;
- BTC dominance falling can allow altcoins to retrace imbalances even if BTC is weak;
- old altcoins may underperform, so each altcoin needs its own HTF levels;
- "buy zone" means area to watch, not automatic entry.

## Reusable Playbook

### Setup: Confirmed Deviation To Opposite Range Edge

- Conditions: established range; sweep outside one edge; liquidity taken.
- Trigger: price returns inside range and consolidates.
- Targets: mid-range, range quarter, opposite range edge, then opposite-side liquidity.
- Invalidation: price accepts outside the range again or weekly close confirms outside acceptance.

### Setup: Order-Book Liquidity Target

- Conditions: order book and heatmap show clear liquidity cluster near chart support/resistance.
- Trigger: price structure aligns with the liquidity direction, such as loss/reclaim of 1h/4h50EMA, range low/high, or trendline.
- Targets: the nearest large liquidity cluster, then next chart support/resistance.
- Invalidation: liquidity is removed/absorbed, price accepts away from target, or macro context flips.

### Setup: Wick Fill With EMA Reclaim

- Conditions: obvious unfilled wick and price reacting at support/resistance.
- Trigger: price reclaims 1h/4h50EMA and retests it as support, or loses/retests as resistance for shorts.
- Targets: 50% wick fill first, full wick second, then nearby liquidity.
- Invalidation: failure to hold the reclaimed EMA or failure to close back inside the range.
