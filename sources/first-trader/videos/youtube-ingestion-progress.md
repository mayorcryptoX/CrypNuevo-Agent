## Evolution So Far

- The strategy starts mature: liquidity, wick fills, session logic, DXY/SPX, Hyblock, and invalidation are present from the first videos.
- Videos 006-010 formalize wick-fill targets: 50% wick fill as minimum, 100% wick fill as full target.
- Daily 50 EMA becomes a major BTC structural pivot; 1h50EMA and 4h50EMA become lower-timeframe execution/context tools.
- Videos 011-025 add macro-event risk filters, BTC dominance for altcoins, and high-timeframe altcoin range preparation.
- Videos 026-040 add the weekly psychological pattern: weekend range, Monday false move, London hold, New York stop-hunt/reversal, and possible midweek reversal.
- Videos 026-040 also add explicit plan-reset behavior after invalidation and a 20/50 weekly MA bull/bear framework.
- Videos 041-055 formalize the range-deviation playbook: range high/low/midpoint/quarters, liquidity sweep, return inside range, consolidation, then target mid-range/opposite-side liquidity.
- Videos 041-055 add explicit CPI-day rules: do not guess the number, wait 30-45 minutes after release, expect a false first move, then trade the reclaim/retest.
- Videos 056-070 expand into a cross-market workflow: DXY/SPX/FX checks, BTC dominance for altcoins, order-book/heatmap liquidity targeting, and weekly candle close as range acceptance confirmation.
- Videos 056-070 also show wick-fill, liquidity pools, and trading sessions are central enough that he mentions building an indicator around them.
- Videos 071-085 were analyzed with chart frames. Visual patterns: turquoise support/liquidity boxes, white/yellow route projections, mid-range magnets, EMA resistance/support, support-box sweeps, and heatmap/order-book confirmation after the TradingView plan.
- Videos 071-085 show news/ETF moves treated as catalysts only when a technical liquidity setup already exists.
- Videos 086-100 separate day-trade execution from swing-trade planning: Sunday update for route, day-trade for session/EMA/wick execution, FOMC for post-event imbalance trading.
- Videos 086-100 refine manipulation/fakeout logic: sweep highs, return below, retest as resistance, then target EMA/lower wick/liquidity; 100% wick fill requires consolidation after the 50% fill instead of rejection.
- Videos 086-095 add a precise execution layer: swing bias and day-trade trigger are separated, FOMC/event candles are traded only after second-structure confirmation, 1h/4h50EMA becomes an explicit trade gate, and 50% wick fill becomes a decision point for whether 100% fill remains likely.
- Videos 086-095 also give a clean manipulation pattern: sweep a psychological/prior high, wait for momentum shift, retest the swept level as resistance, then target nearby 15m50EMA/support rather than assuming unlimited downside.
- Videos 101-115 make stop-loss-zone hunting explicit: mark obvious retail resistance/support, infer where stops sit, then trade only after sweep plus reclaim/rejection confirmation.
- Videos 101-115 refine the 4h50EMA rule into a two-candle gate: for shorts, close below and test as resistance for two candles; for longs, reclaim and hold as support.
- Videos 101-115 add momentum filtering, BTC.D fade logic for altcoin catch-up, and HTF upside liquidation targeting around levels such as 74.1k.
- Videos 116-130 show a format change toward deeper weekly/market-outlook videos: weekly/daily structure first, then 4h/1h triggers.
- Videos 116-130 confirm the CrypNuevo indicator modules: wick-fill levels, liquidity pools, trading sessions, and multi-timeframe 50MA.
- Videos 116-130 add spot-buying and altseason frameworks that require BTC support, ETH/alt relative strength, BTC.D/TOTAL3 alignment, and clear invalidation.
- Videos 131-137 make execution/risk explicit: manual wick-fill management (no hard stop), staged/laddered sizing, order-book spoof check, range quarters, and the weekly 50MA as the bull/bear regime line.
- Videos 138-151 (post-hiatus, current) add the manipulation rationale (exchange-owned market makers; FTX/Alameda), "room for liquidity"/"liquidity trend lines", new tools (options gamma, Hyblock delta, M2 liquidity, mining cost), full TP/stop mechanics, a bullish-2026 macro thesis, and explicit rejection of the four-year cycle.
