## Chart Patterns Observed

This batch adds a sharper execution layer to the previous route-map style. The recurring visual components are:

- yellow horizontal levels for range high, range low, mid-range, and wick-fill checkpoints;
- purple 50 EMA curves used as support/resistance gates on 1h and 4h;
- turquoise boxes for liquidity pools, demand/support boxes, or imbalance zones;
- white arrows that show both scenarios, often pump first then dump, or sweep first then reclaim;
- circles around stop-hunts, retests, failed reclaim areas, or trapped breakout zones;
- Hyblock heatmap/order-book screens used after the TradingView scenario to verify likely liquidity targets.

The most important visual change is the separation of swing logic and day-trade logic. He can be bullish on a swing route while still waiting for an intraday pullback, wick fill, or EMA retest before entry.

## Main Strategy Findings

### Swing Bias And Day-Trade Entry Are Separate

Videos 086 and 087 show that a directional swing idea does not automatically become a market entry.

The swing route is built from:

- mid-range;
- daily/weekly/4h 50 EMA;
- range high and range low;
- major liquidation pools;
- news-event volatility risk.

The day-trade entry is built from:

- New York session behavior;
- 1h/4h 50 EMA reaction;
- recent wick fill;
- immediate liquidity above/below;
- whether the latest candle closes above or below the key EMA/level.

This matters for recognition: if the chart is overextended into resistance, he avoids chasing even when the larger target remains higher.

### FOMC / News Day Rule Becomes Very Procedural

Video 087 is the cleanest FOMC workflow in this group.

Pattern:

1. Do not enter before the event just because a target exists.
2. Expect the first move to be false or incomplete.
3. Let the large event candle create imbalances and liquidity pools.
4. After the release, use 1h/15m structure to identify the leftover pool.
5. Enter only after reclaim/retest/rejection confirms the post-news route.

Invalidation is not the headline. Invalidation is the chart accepting beyond the planned level or EMA after the event.

### Session Sequence: Asia Holds, London Dumps, New York Reverses

Video 088 makes the session model concrete again.

Setup:

- Asia holds a level or range;
- London pushes price into a dump or pump;
- New York is watched for stop-hunt/reversal;
- if New York does not reclaim, the next lower liquidity pool becomes active.

Visual clue: he draws a sharp London move, then a possible New York recovery path. Hyblock is checked for the closest realistic pool, not only the biggest distant pool.

### 4h50EMA Gate For Day Trades

Video 089 gives a precise day-trade gate:

- Price below the 4h50EMA means the EMA is potential resistance.
- A pump into the upper wick/1h50EMA/4h50EMA area can become a short only if it rejects.
- A 4h candle close above the 4h50EMA invalidates the short idea.
- If the 4h50EMA flips into support, the bias changes toward a long/reclaim route.

This is one of the most reusable chart-recognition rules in the dataset.

### Planned Entry, Compound Entry, And Abort Condition

Video 090 shows entry management rather than just direction.

Long-side recovery pattern:

- Start small inside a marked support/liquidity zone.
- If price performs a stop-hunt wick and reacts, compound on confirmation.
- If price consolidates in the box with no bounce, close or stand aside.
- If the lower range level is lost, wait for the next support instead of defending the idea.

Short-side continuation pattern:

- False move above 1h/4h50EMA.
- Close back below the EMA.
- Target lower liquidation/wick area.
- Invalidation is reclaim/acceptance back above the EMA.

### 50% Wick Fill Reaction Decides Whether 100% Fill Is Likely

Video 092 is very useful for wick-fill recognition.

Rules inferred:

- 50% wick fill is a first reaction zone, not automatic completion.
- Hard rejection at 50% can end the idea or trigger reversal.
- Consolidation around 50% after a reclaim increases the odds of a 100% wick fill.
- If price loses 1h50EMA after failing 50%, he looks for a retest-short route toward lower EMA/range support.

This adds a quality filter to the older wick-fill rule from videos 001-025.

### Manipulation Trade: Sweep, Wait, Retest, Then Short

Video 093 is the strongest real example of his manipulation trade.

Pattern:

- Order book shows large visible orders near a psychological level.
- Price sweeps above the level and prior high, attracting breakout longs.
- Orders are removed or the move fails to continue.
- Do not short the vertical move immediately.
- Wait for momentum shift and a retest of the swept level as resistance.
- Short toward the nearest 15m50EMA/liquidity area.
- Take profit at the nearby EMA/support rather than assuming full collapse.

The chart frames show the swept upper box, circles around the prior high/retest area, and the rejection route.

### HTF Range Imbalance Mapping

Videos 094 and 095 shift back to higher-timeframe range planning.

Pattern:

- Mark the active macro range and deviations.
- Mark discount/premium style imbalance boxes.
- Identify whether price is filling a new wick or just ranging inside a larger structure.
- Use order book/heatmap to verify whether upside or downside liquidity is stronger.
- Take profits into HTF resistance after extended weekly green candles.
- Treat deep pullbacks as possible accumulation zones only if broader structure remains valid.

This is not immediate scalping. It is a map of where the next high-quality trade should be prepared.

## Reusable Recognition Rules

### Entry Rules

- Enter after the sweep and reclaim, not on the first touch of the box.
- Enter short after failed reclaim/retest of swept high, not while price is still pumping.
- Enter long after support-box reaction or EMA reclaim, not while price is falling into the box.
- On news days, wait for the post-event structure; the first candle is usually information, not execution.
- For wick fills, the 50% level is a decision point; continuation needs consolidation or acceptance.

### Confirmation Rules

- 1h/4h50EMA reclaim confirms long continuation.
- 1h/4h50EMA rejection confirms short continuation.
- 4h candle close is stronger than intrabar touch for the 4h50EMA gate.
- Hyblock/order-book liquidity should support the TradingView route.
- Session sequence should support the idea: Asia/London move sets the trap, New York confirms or cancels.
- Consolidation above a reclaimed level is stronger than a single wick.

### Exit Rules

- Take partials near mid-range, 50% wick fill, 1h/4h50EMA, or the closest liquidity pool.
- For manipulation shorts, take profit at nearby 15m50EMA/support if that was the planned target.
- Avoid holding weekend positions when the setup has already paid or when liquidity is thin.
- HTF swing targets can stay open only if lower-timeframe invalidation has not fired.

### Invalidation Rules

- Day-trade short invalidated by 4h close above the 4h50EMA when that EMA is the rejection thesis.
- Long invalidated by acceptance below the marked support/liquidity box.
- Wick-fill continuation invalidated by hard rejection at 50% plus loss of 1h50EMA.
- News route invalidated by post-event acceptance beyond the planned range/EMA level.
- Manipulation short invalidated if swept level flips into support and price consolidates above it.
- Alt/HTF buy zones invalidated by BTC weakness plus BTC dominance strength or by clean loss of the mapped range.
