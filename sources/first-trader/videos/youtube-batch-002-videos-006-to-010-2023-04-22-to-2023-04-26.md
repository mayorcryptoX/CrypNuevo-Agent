## Clean Summary

This batch formalizes the wick-fill strategy and moves the early framework from "liquidity exists" to "which imbalance gets hit first?"

The core decision stack:

1. Identify imbalances: long wick, liquidity pool, weekend range.
2. Check daily 50 EMA as major support/resistance.
3. Use lower-timeframe 50 EMA for execution and confirmation.
4. Watch whether price leaves imbalances behind.
5. Use Hyblock liquidation heatmap to decide which target has more incentive.
6. Avoid trading around weekends and macro events without strict risk.

## Strategy Additions

### Wick-Fill Rule Gets Explicit

Video 6 states the clearest early rule:

- Wicks are liquidity imbalances.
- Minimum target is 50% of the wick.
- Final target is 100% of the wick.

This confirms that the later X "wick fill strategy" was already present in April 2023.

### Daily 50 EMA As Main Structural Pivot

Videos 6-10 repeatedly use the daily 50 EMA:

- If BTC holds above the daily 50 EMA, it can remain supported.
- If BTC closes below the daily 50 EMA, lower targets become likely.
- If price sweeps liquidity below but closes back above the daily 50 EMA, that can be a false move / bullish continuation signal.

### One-Hour 50 EMA As Execution Filter

Video 10 explains that even when the target is obvious, entering is difficult if BTC remains below the 1h50EMA resistance. He wants:

- bounce,
- break above 1h50EMA,
- retest it as support,
- then continuation.

This is a more concrete execution trigger than the first batch.

### Weekend Range

Videos 6 and 10 treat the weekend range as low-liquidity chop. The next directional move often comes after the range breaks and targets the nearest wick or liquidity pool.

### Macro Risk Filter

Video 9 begins emphasizing macro data:

- upcoming FOMC,
- consumer/economic data,
- choppy range conditions,
- advice to reduce trades / avoid overtrading.
