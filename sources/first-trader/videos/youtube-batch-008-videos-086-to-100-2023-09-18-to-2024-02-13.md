## Main Strategy Findings

### Day Trade Vs Swing Trade

Video 086 clearly separates day-trading setups from swing-trading setups.

Swing framework:

- Sunday update gives the broad route.
- Mid-range and daily/weekly 50EMA define the main swing decision area.
- Range high and large liquidity pools are larger targets.
- Swing longs need support/reclaim, not blind limit orders.

Day-trade framework:

- shorter-term wick or liquidity pool;
- 1h/4h50EMA retest;
- New York session timing;
- smaller scalp target, often opposite wick/liquidity pool.

This is important: he may be bullish for a swing path while still looking for an intraday short scalp if price hits a wick/EMA resistance.

### FOMC High-Probability Process

Video 087 reinforces the exact FOMC rule:

- rate decision may already be priced in;
- Dot Plot and Powell press conference are the volatility source;
- do not trade the release blindly;
- wait until everything calms;
- trade imbalances created by the move after dust settles.

This is more precise than "avoid FOMC." The rule is: avoid guessing, then trade the imbalance left by the news move if structure confirms.

### Asia / London / New York Execution

Video 088 visually confirms the session model.

Typical route:

- Asia holds or creates the first move;
- London dumps or moves price into the setup zone;
- New York can reverse after liquidity has been taken;
- if a FOMC/news move leaves imbalances, New York often retraces them before the session ends.

The session logic is not standalone. It needs a wick, imbalance, EMA, liquidity pool, or support/resistance confluence.

### 1h / 4h 50EMA As Execution Gate

Videos 089-092 repeat a strict EMA rule:

- below 4h50EMA and testing it as resistance is bad for swing longs;
- reclaim and retest of 1h/4h50EMA can trigger longs;
- wick-fill trades often use the 1h50EMA as retrace/support or resistance;
- a wick target is not enough; he wants the EMA/level behavior.

### 100% Wick Fill Conditions

Video 092 refines the wick-fill rule:

- 50% wick fill can be a trade target and possible reaction zone;
- 100% wick fill becomes likely if price consolidates/holds after 50% fill instead of rejecting;
- if range high flips from resistance to support, 100% wick fill becomes more realistic;
- if price loses the flipped support, the lower liquidity pool/wick remains target.

This is the clearest condition so far for when he upgrades from 50% wick target to 100% wick target.

### Manipulation Trade

Video 093 is a real example of the manipulation setup.

Observed sequence:

1. Price approaches an obvious round level or range high.
2. Retail breakout traders enter.
3. Price sweeps highs/stop losses.
4. Candle closes back under the level.
5. The level is retested as resistance.
6. Short targets are the 15m/1h50EMA, lower wick, or liquidity pool.

Important: he warns against blind limit orders at the level. He wants the "switch in momentum" after the sweep.

### Breakout Or Fakeout Decision Tree

Videos 096-098 visually formalize breakout/fakeout thinking:

- If price breaks range highs and holds/retests support, continuation toward upper liquidity pool is valid.
- If price breaks and returns inside the range, treat it as a fakeout/deviation.
- If price forms a triangle/compression just below a liquidity-pool box, wait for direction.
- If wick-fill target is reached, reassess rather than assuming immediate continuation.

The visual decision tree is usually drawn as:

- white range box;
- turquoise upper liquidity pool;
- turquoise lower support/liquidity pool;
- arrow up into liquidity if reclaim holds;
- arrow down if fakeout returns inside.

### Market Maker Changing To Shorts

Video 100 adds a distribution read:

- after a vertical pump, repeated stair-step consolidation boxes can show market makers shifting from long accumulation into short-building/distribution;
- if price has already taken major upside liquidity, he starts looking for evidence of a short setup;
- still, he waits for a breakdown/retest or failed reclaim instead of shorting only because price is high.

## Exact Setup Library Additions

### Setup: News Imbalance After FOMC

- Conditions: FOMC/CPI/Powell event produces large candles and leaves visible imbalances.
- Entry trigger: wait until press conference/release volatility calms, then trade the imbalance retrace with EMA/range confirmation.
- Targets: 50%/100% imbalance fill, nearby wick, session liquidity.
- Invalidation: price accepts beyond the news impulse without returning.

### Setup: 100% Wick-Fill Continuation

- Conditions: price has already filled 50% of a wick and does not reject strongly.
- Entry trigger: consolidation above support or 1h/4h50EMA; range high flips support; momentum holds.
- Targets: 100% wick fill, then next liquidity pool.
- Invalidation: loss of flipped support or failure to hold EMA.

### Setup: Manipulation Sweep Reversal

- Conditions: obvious round number/range high with breakout interest and liquidity above.
- Entry trigger: sweep high, return below level, retest as resistance.
- Targets: 15m/1h50EMA, lower wick, liquidity pool, range midpoint.
- Invalidation: reclaim and acceptance above the swept level.

### Setup: Breakout/Fakeout Decision Tree

- Conditions: price compresses near range high or liquidity pool.
- Bull trigger: breakout, retest, hold as support.
- Bear trigger: breakout fails, returns inside range, retests resistance.
- Targets: upper liquidity for bull case; lower support/liquidity for bear case.
- Invalidation: whichever route loses its reclaimed/rejected level.
