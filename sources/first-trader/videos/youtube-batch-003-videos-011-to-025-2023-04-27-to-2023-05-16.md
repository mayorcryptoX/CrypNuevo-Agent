## Strategy Additions

### Trading Imbalances After Large Candles

Video 11 discusses a large one-hour BTC candle and says there are two ways to trade the move:

- trade the initial drop,
- or trade the imbalance left behind by the large candle.

This strengthens the "imbalance fill" part of his model beyond just wick fills.

### Liquidity Pool -> Wick Sequence

Video 12 tracks a projection of first hitting a liquidity pool, then moving to a wick. He also warns that a fast move that leaves a wick behind cannot be fully trusted because it may need to retrace that wick.

### Altcoin Framework Appears

Videos 13 and 20 introduce altcoin long-term preparation:

- use high timeframes: daily, weekly, monthly;
- map range high, range low, middle of range;
- identify liquidity pools and imbalances both above and below;
- do not assume the bottom is in;
- think in bull-market preparation zones rather than immediate signals.

### BTC Dominance Filter

Video 16 explains BTC dominance:

- BTC dominance rising means BTC outperforms alts;
- BTC dominance falling means alts are safer / can outperform;
- best altcoin environment is BTC rising while dominance falls;
- if BTC falls and dominance rises, altcoins can make new lows.

This is a clear addition to the X profile: alt trades need BTC.D context.

### FOMC/CPI Rule

Videos 15, 17, and 23 repeatedly warn around FOMC/CPI:

- expect false moves and volatility;
- use strict risk management;
- avoid trading shortly before and after the event;
- wait for the market to reveal direction after the data.

Video 15 gives a concrete rule: he does not like trading 30 minutes before FOMC and 45 minutes after the press conference.

### Daily 50 EMA Lost

Videos 21-22 track daily 50 EMA as a major BTC support that had held multiple times. Losing it changes the structure and raises concern, though he still allows for a fakeout if macro data reverses the move.
