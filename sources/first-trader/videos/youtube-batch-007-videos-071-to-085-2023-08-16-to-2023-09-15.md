## Chart Patterns Observed

This batch was analyzed with chart frames, not just captions. The visual pattern language is consistent:

- turquoise/blue rectangles mark support zones, liquidity zones, and buy/watch zones;
- white horizontal lines mark range highs, range lows, mid-range, and prior support/resistance;
- yellow or white projected paths show the preferred route, often sweep -> reclaim -> pump -> later dump;
- circles highlight traps, wick areas, or precise retests;
- purple/blue EMA curves, especially 1h/4h/daily/weekly 50EMA, act as moving support/resistance;
- after drawing the route on TradingView, he frequently checks Hyblock heatmap/order book to verify where liquidity is concentrated.

The most common visual setup is:

1. Mark a support/liquidity box below price.
2. Draw a sweep or stop-hunt into/through the box.
3. Draw reclaim or sideways consolidation back above it.
4. Draw a squeeze/pump toward mid-range, 50EMA, range high, or wick-fill target.
5. Draw a later rejection/dump path if price reaches resistance and fails.

This appears clearly in videos 071, 081, 082, 083, 084, and 085.

## Main Strategy Findings

### Support Sweep Into Mid-Range

Videos 071-073 and 081-085 repeatedly show a support-box sweep followed by a return toward mid-range.

The visual idea:

- price is sitting near a support or old range low;
- he expects a liquidity sweep/stop-hunt below the support;
- if price reclaims, the target becomes mid-range or the next EMA/resistance;
- if price fails to reclaim, the lower support/wick target remains active.

This is the long-side mirror of his earlier deviation-short setup.

### Market Maker Position Building

He visually identifies market maker accumulation/distribution by repeated wicks that fail to break a level.

Observed pattern:

- repeated lower wicks into the same box can mean longs are being built;
- repeated upper wicks into the same resistance can mean shorts are being built;
- if price approaches a liquidation zone but misses it by a small amount, he often expects liquidity to build there for a later sweep.

Videos 073, 075, 076, and 077 are the strongest examples.

### Mid-Range As Magnet

The mid-range is now a recurring target, not just a reference line.

Rules inferred from captions and visuals:

- after range-low support holds, mid-range is the first serious magnet;
- if price is below a former range and reclaims support, mid-range becomes a logical take-profit area;
- weekly/daily/4h50EMA near mid-range strengthens the target/resistance zone;
- he sometimes recommends taking profit slightly before the exact mid-range if a higher-timeframe EMA sits just below it.

### ETF / News Interpretation

Video 078 is mostly explanatory rather than chart-driven. The key trading rule is that narrative pumps are still judged through the same liquidity/EMA/range lens.

He says the Grayscale/ETF news may have accelerated the move, but the chart setup for a short squeeze was already there. This is important: news is treated as catalyst, not as the strategy.

### Altcoin Buy-Zone Visual Method

Video 074 shows the same high-timeframe preparation style from earlier altcoin videos:

- draw large support boxes below current price;
- mark old resistance/support flips;
- mark wedge/trendline breakdowns;
- wait for price to reach the zone or reclaim it;
- do not treat the box as an automatic buy.

The altcoin charts are mostly HTF zone maps. Immediate execution is left conditional on BTC, BTC dominance, and reclaim behavior.

### Weekly Update Structure

Videos 079 and 081 show a more mature weekly update format:

1. Check calendar and low-liquidity conditions.
2. Check weekly candle and long wicks.
3. Rebuild the BTC range map.
4. Draw primary and alternate paths.
5. Check DXY/SPX and broader risk if relevant.
6. Check heatmap/order book for liquidity confirmation.

The weekly route is visually drawn as a multi-leg path, not a single direction call.

## Reusable Playbook

### Setup: Support-Box Sweep To Mid-Range

- Conditions: price is near a known support, old range low, or HTF buy/watch box.
- Visual clue: turquoise box under price, white/yellow path dipping into or below it.
- Trigger: sweep/stop-hunt into the box, then reclaim or hold above it.
- Targets: 1h/4h50EMA, mid-range, daily/weekly 50EMA, range high, or wick fill.
- Invalidation: failure to reclaim the box or acceptance below support.

### Setup: Market Maker Missed-Liquidity Trap

- Conditions: price misses a major liquidation/heatmap zone by a small amount and then consolidates away from it.
- Visual clue: heatmap bright zone remains just beyond recent high/low; TradingView chart shows consolidation after the miss.
- Thesis: liquidity is being built for a later sweep.
- Trigger: price begins moving back toward the missed zone with structure/EMA support.
- Target: the missed liquidity zone.
- Invalidation: liquidity disappears or price accepts away from the zone.

### Setup: News Catalyst With Pre-Existing Technical Setup

- Conditions: FOMC minutes, CPI, ETF, or macro catalyst arrives while a liquidity setup already exists.
- Rule: do not chase the headline; ask whether the chart had a squeeze/sweep setup before the news.
- Trigger: after news volatility, wait for reclaim/retest or deviation confirmation.
- Targets: same technical targets as before the news.
- Invalidation: news move breaks structure and holds beyond the planned invalidation.
